Rental Returns

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

What data the app handles

Rental Returns handles the return notes, delivery notes and photos you create in the app (such as prop descriptions, hire company details, dates and photos of returned items), plus basic Google account information needed to sign in.

Where data is stored

All data is stored only in your own Google Drive. The app does not keep copies on any other server.

Use of Google APIs

The app's access to Google APIs is limited to Google Drive files that the app creates or that you open with it. It does not read other files in your Drive.

Rental Returns' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

Your data is not shared with third parties, not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI or machine learning models.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already saved remain in your Drive under your control.
